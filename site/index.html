How to read it
KM Kernel Momentum (Baz normalized MACD, SuperSmoother 4/12 · 8/24 · 16/48). Green LONG ≥ +0.3σ, red SHORT ≤ −0.3σ, gray FLAT. Dashed gold = stretched beyond ±2σ. Glow = flipped in the last 3 bars.
VR Variance Ratio regime (k 8, 120 bars, z 1.96). TREND = moves follow through, CHOP = moves reverse, RAND = no edge.
FLOW Normalized order flow (5/15/45), in σ. Delta is estimated from where each bar closes in its range: free data has no real bid/ask volume, so treat it as a proxy. Blank for forex and indices (no volume).
BBWP Bollinger Band Width Percentile (7, lookback 100). Blue ≤ 12 = squeeze (expansion coming), red ≥ 90 = already expanded.
σ TODAY HAR-RV forecast of today's 1σ move. ↑ = forecast well above the EWMA (vol rising: size down). DAY POS = price vs today's open in σ; beyond ±2σ the day's move is used up.
LIQUIDITY Nearest untouched PDH / PDL / PWH / PWL and its distance in daily σ (+ above, − below). Orange = a level swept and reclaimed today.
PLAYBOOK A trend / A pullback = 1H KM has a side; B range fade = FLAT + CHOP; C break-retest = FLAT + TREND; wait = FLAT + RAND.
SCORE 0–10: how much agrees with the 1H side (KM on all four timeframes, flow on 15m and 1H, 15m VR trend, not beyond ±2σ). A filter for what to open on TradingView, not a signal.
